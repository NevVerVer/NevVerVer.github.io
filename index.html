25–29 Jun 2024

FENS Forum 2024

Vienna, Austria

ePoster on our Scientific Reports paper about jPCA rotations in motor cortex activity. We showed that a travelling wave in neuronal firing is enough to produce rotations, and introduced the gyration number, a measure that compares rotational structure across datasets without dimensionality reduction. Tested on 59 recordings from five datasets.

Sep 2022

MICCAI 2022

Singapore

Poster on Autofocusing+, a method that removes motion artefacts from MRI. A neural network prior is added to classic autofocusing: the optimization iteratively undoes the motion, while the network penalizes unrealistic restorations and speeds up convergence. The method stays robust to noise and outperformed state-of-the-art methods.

Sep 2022

Baltic Forum BF-NAICS

Kaliningrad

Oral: Further observations on the rotational structure in neural data, part of the jPCA rotations project. Grant winner in the competition for young scientists.
